About Fix My Speaker

Introduction

Fix My Speaker is a free website that plays sounds designed to help clear water and dust from phone and computer speakers.

Our tools

A water eject sound, a dust removal sound, and a set of speaker tests.

How it works

Your browser plays audio files and generates test tones locally. Sound vibration may help move water or dust, but results vary.

Safety

We recommend moderate volume and clearly state that the tool cannot repair hardware.

Privacy

The tools do not record or upload audio. See the Privacy Policy.

Mission

To offer a simple, honest, free speaker tool.
